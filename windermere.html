What makes Windermere pools different

Scale and complexity. A typical Windermere custom pool might run a spa with its own jet loop, a spillover, a waterfall or sheer descent, sun-shelf bubblers, and an in-floor cleaning system — each adding buried lines and fittings beyond what a standard pool carries. That multiplies the places a leak can hide and the lines a thorough test has to cover. Lakefront lots on the Butler Chain add a high water table, and generously irrigated landscaping can keep soil damp enough to muddy the “wet ground” clue. On properties like these, systematic line-by-line testing is not a luxury; it is the only way to get a trustworthy answer.

Leak patterns we hear about from Windermere

Spa-versus-pool asymmetries lead the list: a spa that drains overnight while the pool holds, or vice versa, which immediately narrows the search to one circuit. Feature-linked loss is next — a pool that drops noticeably more on days the waterfall runs. And on negative-edge and spillover designs, owners sometimes struggle to tell normal catch-basin fluctuation from real loss; that is a fair confusion, and exactly the kind of thing the follow-up call can talk through before anyone visits.

Windermere pool leak questions

The spa loses water overnight but the pool seems fine. What does that mean?

Usually a leak in the spa’s own circuit — its plumbing, jets, or the spillover assembly — or a check valve letting the spa drain back toward the pool when the pump stops. Because pool-spa combinations share equipment but run separate loops, “spa drops, pool holds” is actually a helpfully specific clue. Mention it exactly that way.

Do waterfalls and water features leak?

Their plumbing can, and it is easy to miss because feature lines often run long routes and only carry water when the feature runs. A pool that loses extra water on days the waterfall was on points squarely at the feature circuit — one more line to pressure test, and a common finding in larger custom pools.

We’re in a gated community — does that complicate the visit?

Only logistically. A gate code or guard-house heads-up when scheduling is all it takes. It has no effect on testing or pricing.
