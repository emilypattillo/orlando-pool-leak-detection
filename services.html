Pool Leak Detection Orlando helps Central Florida pool owners with the full path of a leak problem — finding it, confirming it, and repairing what the test locates. The services below share one starting point: no repair is recommended until a test shows where the water is actually going.

Pool leak detection

The core service. Using pressure testing, dye testing, and electronic listening, a leak test locates where a full pool is losing water — the plumbing, the shell, or a fitting — without draining it. This is where every job begins, because guessing at a repair on a pool is an expensive way to be wrong.

Pool plumbing leak detection

When the loss is in the lines, the suction and return plumbing is pressure-tested and isolated so the leak is narrowed to a specific run, often under the deck or slab. Air at the returns or a pump that keeps losing prime usually points here.

Pool leak repair

Once the leak is located, the repair is matched to it — a fitting or seal, a section of plumbing, or a crack in the shell. You get the finding and the fix together, rather than a vague "we'll seal it up."

Skimmer, light, and fitting leaks

Some of the most common Central Florida leaks are at the skimmer throat, the pool light niche and conduit, and the returns. These are checked directly with dye and pressure testing because they hide well and mimic evaporation.

How the services fit together

  • Start with detection — confirm the loss and locate it.
  • Match the repair to the finding — no blind fixes.
  • Keep the pool full and in service throughout wherever possible.

Not sure which service you need? That's normal. Request a leak callback or call and describe what you're seeing, and the right starting point gets sorted out on the call.

Common questions

Questions about services

Which service do I start with?

Almost always detection. Locating the leak first means the repair is aimed at the actual problem instead of a guess.

Can you do detection and repair together?

Yes. The test locates the leak and the repair is matched to what it finds, so you get both the finding and the fix.

Do you handle both plumbing and shell leaks?

Yes. Testing distinguishes a plumbing-line leak from a shell, skimmer, light, or fitting leak, and the repair follows the finding.
