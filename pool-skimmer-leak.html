Skimmer leaks

Pool Skimmer Leak Detection & Repair in Orlando, FL

The skimmer is one of the most common — and most quietly costly — leak points on a Central Florida pool. Here's how a skimmer leak is confirmed and why catching it early pays off.

Pool Leak Detection Orlando checks and repairs skimmer leaks for Orlando-area pools — one of the most common and most overlooked leak points in Central Florida. The skimmer sits right at the waterline, so a crack or separation there tends to lose water steadily and quietly, often mistaken for ordinary evaporation until the level parks just below the skimmer mouth.

What a skimmer leak looks like

Skimmers leak at the throat (where the plastic skimmer meets the concrete shell) and at the body and its plumbing connections. Years of sun, ground movement, and pool chemistry separate that bond over time. A telltale sign is a pool that drops until it reaches the bottom of the skimmer opening and then holds.

How a skimmer leak is confirmed

  • Dye testing at the skimmer throat to watch for water drawing into a crack.
  • Checking the skimmer's plumbing connections for separate loss.
  • Comparing the loss pattern against the skimmer waterline.

Why skimmer leaks are worth catching early

Because they mimic evaporation, skimmer leaks often run for months — wasting water and chemistry the whole time, and letting water work into the deck seam beside the skimmer. Caught early, the repair is usually contained to the skimmer area rather than the surrounding deck.

What affects the scope

Whether the leak is at the throat, the body, or the plumbing changes the repair. Access and the skimmer's condition factor in too. Scope is confirmed on a call after the test.

What happens after you call or request a leak callback

Describe the level where the drop stops and any cracking you can see, and the skimmer test gets scoped. You'll get the finding and the recommended repair together.

Common questions

Questions about skimmer leaks

How do I know it's the skimmer?

A common sign is a pool that drops to the bottom of the skimmer opening and then stops. Dye testing confirms whether the throat or body is leaking.

Is a skimmer leak a big repair?

Caught early, it's usually contained to the skimmer area. Left for months, water can work into the adjacent deck, which enlarges the job.

Could it just be evaporation?

It can look identical, which is why it's tested rather than assumed. A level and dye test separates the two.
