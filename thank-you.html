While you wait

If the water level is dropping quickly, it is fine to keep adding water so the level stays above the skimmer opening — letting it fall below the skimmer can run the pump dry and damage it. Other than that, normal pool use is usually fine until the leak is found.

If the situation feels urgent — water dropping several inches a day, soggy ground near equipment, or a pump losing prime — call (321) 972-8852 directly instead of waiting for the follow-up.
