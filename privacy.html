Last updated July 02, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Pool Leak Detection Orlando ("we," "us," or "this site") collects, how it is used, and how it is shared. This is a local marketing site for pool leak detection and repair in the Orlando, Florida area. By using this site or submitting the quote form, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We collect the information you provide through the quote form or by phone — typically your name, email, phone number, city or service area, the service you're interested in, and a description of your pool issue. We may also collect basic technical information such as the page you submitted from and general analytics data.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and arrange pool leak detection or repair service.
  • To follow up by phone, text, or email about your inquiry.
  • To route your request to the independent service operator who will contact you.
  • To improve this site and understand which pages are useful.

How we share your information — lead sharing

This is important, so we state it plainly: when you submit a request, your contact details and the description of your issue are shared with one or more independent pool service operators so they can contact you, scope the work, and provide pricing. That is the purpose of the form. We do not sell your information for unrelated marketing, and we share only what's needed to respond to and fulfill your request. The operator who contacts you is responsible for their own handling of your information once it is shared.

Text and call consent

By submitting the form or calling, you consent to be contacted about your request by phone, text, or email, including by automated means where applicable. Consent to marketing texts is not a condition of any service, and message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of texts by replying STOP, or ask to be removed from follow-up at any time.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use standard cookies and analytics to measure traffic and improve content. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data retention and your choices

We keep request information as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record-keeping. To ask what we hold, correct it, or have it removed, contact us using the phone number on this site.

Changes to this policy
